# Literature workflow validation — 2026-09-13

The production changes passed the full 776-test desktop/backend regression suite, 16 Python MCP tests, and subsequent focused selection/scoring tests. The local MCP executable was rebuilt and tested over stdio. These checks establish behavior and integration, not live LLM quality.

## Anonymous live collection

The four predeclared query plans ran through the rebuilt local MCP, followed by one cached next page. No model, provider keys or paid service was used in this lane.

| Request | Unique candidate pool | Inspected | Collection time | Cached page time | Failed provider/query jobs |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| AI and synthetic biology | 136 | 40 | 24.99 s | 2 ms | 8 / 15 |
| Enzyme engineering methods | 94 | 40 | 35.51 s | 2 ms | 5 / 10 |
| Chinese, date-constrained single-cell models | 21 | 21 | 35.51 s | <1 ms | 5 / 10 |
| Sparse EctD evidence | 49 | 40 | 33.78 s | 1 ms | 4 / 10 |

The 141 inspected records had no repeated DOI/handle identities within a request. This does not establish absence of different-DOI versions of the same study. Returned records include incidental matches, reviews, supplements and non-paper items; candidate count is not relevance. Date filtering remains post-retrieval, so a date-constrained pool can be much smaller than the fetched set. Provider rate limits and deadlines reduced coverage. Collection time includes the bounded wait grace; end-to-end collection/startup/page time was approximately 26–36 seconds. These are one-run observations, not a statistically established speed improvement over the previous version.

## Controlled workflow replay

Synthetic records and scripted model choices exercised the actual Agent harness. Three cases reached their requested saved counts; the sparse-evidence case correctly reported one of three saved with a missing-subtopic gap. The broad-topic case recovered from a failed download using a relevant reserve. Selection contained no duplicate identities; relevance and coverage matched the hand-authored fixtures. These are control checks, not measured live-model precision. Simulated download success was 75% in the recovery case and 100% for attempted files in the other cases.

## Remaining live evaluation

The workspace did not provide an LLM transport credential for the live selector. Consequently live model relevance, topic coverage, download yield, total model latency and cost are unmeasured. The benchmark supports this lane with the configured model and reviewed DOI-keyed labels; token-based cost estimates also require explicit model prices. Unknown values remain `null`. No claim of a quantified live selection-quality improvement is made from scripted fixtures or provider candidate counts.
