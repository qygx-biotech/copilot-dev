# Research Question

Evaluation of the compressive modulus and shape retention of a hydrogel formulation consisting of sodium alginate and cellulose.

# Main Findings

- The average compressive modulus of the material is 48.0 kPa.
- The shape retention rate of the material is 94%.
- The average compressive modulus is 48.0 kPa with a sample standard deviation of 3.0 kPa (n=6).
- The shape retention rate is 94%.

# Methods

- Preparation of a formulation with 2.0% (w/v) sodium alginate and 0.8% (w/v) cellulose
- Crosslinking using 50 mmol/L calcium chloride for 10 minutes
- Equilibration in a wet state for 1 hour
- Measurement of compressive modulus at 15% strain

# Limitations

- Differences in strain (15% vs 10%) and equilibrium duration (1 hour vs 24 hours) compared to P17 prevent assertions of superior material performance
- Water absorption rate, cell viability, and cost were not reported

# Short Summary

This study reports that a hydrogel formulation of 2.0% sodium alginate and 0.8% cellulose, crosslinked with 50 mmol/L calcium chloride, exhibits an average compressive modulus of 48.0 kPa at 15% strain and a shape retention rate of 94%.
