# Research Question

Determination of the properties of mutant T212S using tetrahydropyrimidine as a substrate.

# Main Findings

- Mutant T212S has a Km of 0.55 mM when using tetrahydropyrimidine as a substrate at a measurement temperature of 35°C.
- Under cultivation at 35°C for 48 hours, the hydroxy-tetrahydropyrimidine titer is 4.10 g/L, specific activity is 2.20 U/mg, product yield is 0.50 g/g substrate, and volumetric productivity is 0.0854166667 g/L/h.
- Compared to P117, the titer is lower, but the yield and specific activity are higher.
- The Km for mutant T212S with tetrahydropyrimidine is 0.55 mM at 35°C.
- Hydroxy-tetrahydropyrimidine titer reached 4.10 g/L after 48 hours of cultivation at 35°C.

# Methods

- Cultivation at 35°C for 48 hours

# Limitations

- Temperature difference of 5°C compared to P117
- Lack of validation under identical temperature and conditions
- No results reported for 42°C cultivation

# Short Summary

This paper reports the characterization of mutant T212S using tetrahydropyrimidine as a substrate at 35°C, providing data on Km, titer, specific activity, yield, and productivity, and comparing these results to P117.
