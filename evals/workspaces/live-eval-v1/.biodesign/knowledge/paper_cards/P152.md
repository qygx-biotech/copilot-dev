# Main Findings

- T212S leads in specific activity while A163V leads in product titer at 42°C.
- At 42°C, A163V exhibited a specific activity of 1.50 U/mg and a product titer of 4.60 g/L.
- At 42°C, T212S exhibited a specific activity of 2.40 U/mg and a product titer of 3.90 g/L.

# Methods

- synthetic protocol

# Limitations

- Yield and productivity were not reported for these 42°C conditions.
- These synthetic data do not establish a universally superior variant, optimal temperature, or stability beyond the tested condition.

# Short Summary

A comparison of A163V and T212S variants at 42°C for the production of 羟基四氢嘧啶 showed that T212S had higher specific activity (2.40 U/mg) while A163V had a higher product titer (4.60 g/L).
