# Research Question

What are the kinetic and fermentation properties of the EctD variant A163V in the catalysis of ectoine hydroxylation?

# Main Findings

- Variant A163V exhibits a lower Michaelis constant (Km) for ectoine compared to the wild-type enzyme.
- Fermentation using variant A163V at 30°C for 48 hours produced a product titer of 4.80 g/L.
- The specific activity of variant A163V was 1.80 U/mg.
- The product yield for variant A163V was 0.40 g/g substrate.
- The volumetric productivity for variant A163V was 0.100 g/L/h.

# Methods

- Kinetic assay
- Fermentation

# Limitations

- The study did not test 35°C or 42°C cultivation.
- Lower Km alone does not prove higher whole-process productivity.
- No universal best variant is established.

# Short Summary

Characterization of EctD variant A163V showing a reduced Km for ectoine (0.42 mM vs 0.80 mM for wild-type) and specific fermentation metrics including a titer of 4.80 g/L at 30°C.
